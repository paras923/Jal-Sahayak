Irrigation Techniques

Water-Saving Irrigation Methods

💧

Drip Irrigation (Tapak Sinchai)

Delivers water directly to the root zone, reducing consumption by 35–50% vs flood irrigation. Ideal for groundnut, cotton, vegetables. 95%+ water-use efficiency.

Learn More → PMKSY
🚿

Sprinkler Irrigation (Chhantakav)

Simulates rainfall for even water distribution. Saves 20–30% water. Best for wheat, bajra, jowar, and field crops with undulating terrain.

Learn More → PMKSY
🌱

Mulching (Aavaran)

Covering soil with crop residue or plastic reduces evaporation by 30–40%, suppresses weeds, and improves soil moisture retention between irrigations.

India Water Portal
📐

Laser Land Levelling

Precision land levelling using GPS/laser technology improves irrigation efficiency by 20–30%, reduces water-logging and ensures uniform crop stand.

Agri Ministry
🌾

Drought-Resistant Varieties

GG-20, TAG-24 (groundnut); NH-615 (wheat); GHB-732 (bajra) are drought-tolerant varieties recommended for Saurashtra by SAU Junagadh.

SAU Junagadh
🏗️

Farm Pond Construction (Khet Talavdi)

On-farm ponds (20×20×3m) harvest runoff for supplemental irrigation. Under PMKSY, 50% subsidy available to small/marginal farmers in Saurashtra.

PMKSY Portal
Government Schemes

Key Water & Agriculture Schemes

🌊 PMKSY — Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana ▼

India's flagship irrigation scheme: 'Har Khet Ko Pani, More Crop Per Drop.' Provides 55% subsidy on drip/sprinkler installation for small farmers, farm pond construction support, and watershed development.

Visit PMKSY Portal →
💧 Jal Shakti Abhiyan — Catch the Rain ▼

National campaign for water conservation and rainwater harvesting focusing on 256 water-stressed districts. Saurashtra's all 11 districts are covered. Activities include check dam repair, rooftop rainwater harvesting, and groundwater recharge wells.

Jal Shakti Ministry →
🏛️ Sardar Sarovar Project — Narmada Canal Network ▼

The Saurashtra Branch Canal from Sardar Sarovar brings Narmada water to Saurashtra, supplementing groundwater. The SAUNI Yojana (Saurashtra Narmada Avataran Irrigation) fills 115 reservoirs across Saurashtra. Farmers in command areas can register for canal water allocation.

SAUNI Yojana →
🌱 SPSJSY — Sardar Patel Sahkari Jal Sanchay Yojana ▼

Gujarat's landmark check dam movement that built 113,738+ check dams, 240,000+ farm ponds, and 55,917 bori-bandhs statewide. Doubled monsoon recharge in Saurashtra. Communities can apply to build new structures or repair existing ones under WASMO (Water & Sanitation Management Organisation).

Gujarat WASMO →
📋 DPAP — Drought Prone Area Development Programme ▼

All 11 Saurashtra districts are classified under DPAP, making them eligible for drought-specific relief, development works, and watershed programmes under the Ministry of Rural Development's Integrated Watershed Management Programme (IWMP).

Ministry of Rural Dev →

About & Data Sources

Methodology, data provenance, citations, and platform disclaimer

🎯 Platform Purpose

Jal Sahayak is a research and advisory tool designed to help farmers, local bodies, and water authorities in Saurashtra, Gujarat understand drought risk and groundwater depletion. It aggregates publicly available research data, live weather APIs, and rule-based analytics to provide actionable water management guidance.

⚠️ This is an advisory tool only. It is NOT a substitute for official CGWB, IMD, or state government data. Always consult your local agricultural officer or KVK before major farming decisions.

🔢 Drought Risk Index Methodology

The composite drought index (0–100) is calculated as:

Index = (Rainfall Deficit % × 0.35)
      + (GW Depth Increase % × 0.25)
      + (Well Depletion % × 0.25)
      + (CGWB Stress Level × 5)
Thresholds: 0–25 = Low · 25–50 = Moderate · 50–75 = Severe · 75+ = Extreme
